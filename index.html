Settings 1 sheet

Your name, your hourly rate, your tax brackets, your annual revenue goal, your preferred currency. You enter them one time. 👥

Client Management 2 sheets

Clients. Your entire client database in one tidy table. Every contact, email, industry, and hourly rate at a glance. The moment you log a new invoice, this sheet instantly recalculates total billed and total paid for that client. No calculator. No waiting.

Client Profitability. The ranked list that answers one brutally honest question: which clients actually make you real money per hour. The sheet calculates your effective rate on every client, so you know who to double down on and who to politely let go.

🧾

Invoicing 3 sheets

Invoice Tracker (200 rows). Log any invoice in seconds. The client name auto fills from your client sheet. The due date auto calculates from your payment terms. Years of invoices, searchable, sorted, in one quiet place.

Invoice Template. A clean, print ready invoice page. Fill it in, press print or export to PDF, send it to your client in under 60 seconds. Professional. Branded by you. Zero third party apps required.

Late Payment Tracker. The sheet that watches every unpaid invoice for you. The second one goes past its due date, it flags red. It even counts the exact number of days overdue, so you know who to politely nudge first.

💰

Income & Expenses 3 sheets

Income Log. Every payment you receive gets a row. The sheet instantly reserves your tax portion the moment you log the payment. By December, you already have a crystal clear picture of what you owe and what is actually yours.

Expense Tracker. Log any expense. The sheet instantly knows whether it is 100 percent deductible or just 50 percent (hello, business meals) and calculates your deductible amount automatically. You never leave money on the table at tax time.

Recurring Income. A beautiful 12 month grid for your retainer clients. One click per month to mark paid or unpaid. You see the whole year of recurring revenue at a single glance.

📊

Tax & Financial Reports 3 sheets

Tax Estimator. Self employment tax, Federal income tax, State income tax, and VAT. All four, calculated for you in one live estimate. The sheet also tells you the exact dollar amount you still need to set aside this quarter. April panic becomes a thing of your past.

Annual P&L Report. A formal profit and loss statement that updates every time you log an invoice or expense. Share it with your accountant. Submit it with a loan application. Zero manual entry, ever.

Monthly Summary. A 12 month breakdown of income, expenses, and profit. Every single number is auto linked, so you always see your real monthly truth, not a guess.

⏱️

Time & Project Tracking 3 sheets

Projects & Time. A project tracker that pulls each client's hourly rate from the Clients sheet, so your billable value calculates itself the moment you log a project.

Time Log (250 rows). Enter the start time and the end time. The sheet calculates your hours (end minus start, times 24) and your billable value (hours times rate) in the same keystroke. Two hundred and fifty rows of effortless, honest time tracking.

Proposals & Contracts. Track every proposal you send. The sheet calculates your win rate and total pipeline value automatically. You finally see the pattern behind your best wins.

📈

Planning & Forecasting 4 sheets

Rate Calculator. Enter your living costs and your profit target. The sheet tells you the minimum viable hourly rate you must charge to actually live well. One formula ends the lifelong question of "am I charging enough?".

Cashflow Forecast. A 12 month cash projection that updates the second you log a new invoice or expense. Finally see if next quarter will be rich or tight, before it ever arrives.

Goal Tracker. Set your monthly revenue targets. Visual progress bars show you exactly where you stand on each one. Nothing motivates you like watching that bar fill up.

Mileage & Travel. Log every business trip. The sheet calculates your deductible amount at the current IRS mileage rate. Every drive. Every dollar you deserve back at tax time.

📊

Dashboard 2 sheets

Live Dashboard. Six real time charts. Income versus expenses. Net profit. Expense breakdown. Cashflow trend. Revenue by client. Income by service type. Every chart redraws itself the second you type anywhere else inside the file.

Health Scorecard. Five business health KPIs, each scored for you with a green check (✅) or an amber warning (⚠️). Your profit margin. Your collection ratio. Your goal progress. Your effective hourly rate. Your tax reserve coverage. One glance. Full honesty. No more guessing.
