THE PROBLEM

Freelancing Gets Messy When Everything Lives in Different Places.

Notes in one app. Invoices in another. Tax guesses in a third. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Scattered clients

Contact info and project history spread across apps and inboxes.

Unorganized projects

Hard to see what's active, what's waiting, and what's shipped.

Missed deadlines

Important dates slip through when there's no single source of truth.

Unclear revenue

No visibility into how much you've earned — or will earn — this month.

Tax guesswork

SE tax, federal, state, VAT — guessed instead of calculated.

COMPLETE 21-SHEET SYSTEM

21 Sheets. 5,163 Formulas. One Living Spreadsheet.

Set your rates and tax once in Settings — every one of the 21 sheets updates automatically. Yellow cells = you type. Green cells = calculated for you.

⚙️

Settings

1 sheet
  • Settings — Name, tax rates, hourly rate, annual goal, currency. Change once, all 21 sheets update automatically.
👥

Client Management

2 sheets
  • Clients — Full client database. Total billed & paid auto-calculated from invoices.
  • Client Profitability — Who makes you the most money? Effective rate/hour auto-calculated.
🧾

Invoicing

3 sheets
  • Invoice Tracker (200 rows) — Client name auto-fills, due date auto-calculates.
  • Invoice Template — Print-ready, send directly to clients.
  • Late Payment Tracker — Auto-flags every overdue invoice; days overdue auto-counted.
💰

Income & Expenses

3 sheets
  • Income Log — Tax reserve auto-calculated on every payment received.
  • Expense Tracker — Deductible amount auto-set (meals 50%, others 100%).
  • Recurring Income — 12-month paid/unpaid grid for retainer clients.
📊

Tax & Financial Reports

3 sheets
  • Tax Estimator — SE tax + Federal + State + VAT, fully automatic.
  • Annual P&L Report — Formal income statement, zero manual entry.
  • Monthly Summary — 12-month breakdown, all auto-linked.
⏱️

Time & Project Tracking

3 sheets
  • Projects & Time — Rate auto-pulls from client sheet.
  • Time Log (250 rows) — Hours = (end − start) × 24, auto-calculated.
  • Proposals & Contracts — Win rate & pipeline value auto-calculated.
📈

Planning & Forecasting

4 sheets
  • Rate Calculator — Minimum viable hourly rate from your living costs + profit target.
  • Cashflow Forecast — 12-month cash projection linked from Monthly Summary.
  • Goal Tracker — Monthly targets vs actuals with visual progress bars.
  • Mileage & Travel — IRS rate deduction auto-calculated per trip.

Dashboard

2 sheets
  • Live Dashboard — 6 live charts: income vs expenses, net profit, expense breakdown, cashflow trend, revenue by client, income by service type.
